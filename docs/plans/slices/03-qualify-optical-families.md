## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The larger coverage trial has a frozen manifest and acceptance policy before calls. Qualification requires every declared entry and family check to pass; a failed trial blocks 04 for that manifest.
- [ ] Draft measurements, published rules and inferred judgments are distinguishable; independent automated qualification admits revisions with explicit uncertainty.
- [ ] Pilot findings inform a new frozen coverage roster; previously exposed cases are regression data. Completion and novel-family tasks use their separately defined reference exclusions.
- [ ] Trusted Studio selection survives the tool boundary; unauthorized or model-tampered revisions fail before calls.
- [ ] Browser verification covers two styles, refresh, downloaded SVG, exact replay, cancellation, unsupported capability and legacy output display.
- [ ] Required optical masters have their own supported construction; display scaling alone is not reported as a separate generated master. Finishes are required only where declared.
- [ ] Named host operations or pinned AI-generated parts express required contour/optical corrections; model output cannot introduce free path geometry.
- [ ] Judging uses style-specific calibration, semantic controls and wrong-style controls rather than a transplanted Central threshold.
- [ ] Every qualification concept receives a visible verdict in native-size, light/dark and typography context; unresolved defects and unsupported cases stay in the denominator.
- [ ] Costs, failed attempts, unknown costs, elapsed time and automated repairs are recorded. No manual artwork corrections are required; a real run has an explicit total ceiling.
- [ ] A part correction reveals affected family members and a before/after specimen diff; qualification approval identifies exact hashes.
- [ ] The instrument passes its declared defect-sensitivity and false-rejection protocol. Separate repair and sealed evaluators plus deterministic checks drive bounded automatic repair. Unresolved disagreement/low confidence rejects the item without a required human drawing or approval step.
- [ ] Retrieved or reconstructed source icons are labeled separately and do not satisfy new-icon generation criteria.
