A concept request produces an editable outlined/filled icon pair at native16 or native24, with automatically retrieved house references, coherent construction, clean cutouts, independent AI inspection, bounded repair, exact replay and reliable local delivery. Recognition labels, craft judgments and adjudication will come from blinded independent AI instruments plus objective geometric evidence. ## 2. Strategy and consequential decisions

Use **family-aware reference selection → semantic construction → deterministic geometry → native rendering → independent inspection → localized repair → verified delivery**.

An LLM is useful for deciding the metaphor, component arrangement, semantic roles and which defect to repair. The host is responsible for curves, stroke expansion, Boolean topology, rasterization, validation, deadlines and evidence. An image model is a bounded experimental contender only if its result can become editable constrained DSL without losing the required craft. Direct path generation is not the production escape hatch for a representation gap.

| Decision | Evidence and experiment it implies |
| --- | --- |
| Study construction, not only stroke and radius | The [local Blode/Central study](../references/blode-central-craft.md) identifies blended shoulders, optical counters, mixed detail weight and modifier occlusion. Validate those mechanisms across the eight construction classes. |
| Retain structured construction and test refinement | [Chat2SVG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16602) combines primitive templates with geometric refinement. Its published method motivates a comparison; it does not validate this DSL or house parity. |
| Render feedback must demonstrate benefit | [Render-in-the-Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20730) reports that a naïve visual loop is insufficient without suitable training. Measure local repair uplift rather than assuming extra calls improve quality. |
| Keep target reconstruction separate | [RefineSVG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27699) uses target-image residual feedback and training. Target residuals may be used in reconstruction experiments; a hidden house target must never become the objective in novel generation. |
| Qualify the actual critic | [SVG-Score](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03806) reports uneven sensitivity of metrics and VLM judges to SVG errors. Similarity is diagnostic; it is not the craft reward or acceptance authority. |
| Build separate optical masters | [Material Symbols](https://developers.google.com/fonts/docs/material_symbols) documents an optical-size axis. This supports the practice; Blode/Central measurements determine our actual geometry. |

These primary sources were checked while writing this plan. The local study is dated evidence: later compiler18–21 work supersedes its old source-quantization limitation. Historical research was checked through `ccs` in both `iconsmith` and `icon-forge`, then against [the research history](../project-research-history.md); old assistant conclusions are not new experimental findings.

## 3. Exact definition of 10/10

Freeze thresholds, denominators, aggregation, synonyms, controls and populations before qualification. Every row below must pass. A strong average cannot conceal a failed paint, size or adequately sampled construction class.

| Dimension | Required gate | Evidence population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition | At least 95% correct blind recognition in each paint/size stratum; zero accepted conflicting actions | Full catalog critic coverage, independent stratified catalog audit, and each novel batch separately |
| Craft | At least 95% ship unchanged; median calibrated AI craft at least 9/10 | Independent catalog audit and each novel batch; qualified critic on every catalog output |
| House parity | Median paired candidate-minus-house craft at least −0.5/10; at least 95% family-fit approval | Blind matched reference comparisons, separately by paint/size; novel comparisons use withheld evaluation references or predeclared style neighbors |
| Native quality | At least 95% legibility per native size and paint on both light and dark; no accepted collapsed counters, lost modifiers or broken bridges | Exact native masters, not a24 master resized and relabeled16 |
| Geometry | Every accepted output parses, passes mandatory checks and exactly replays; zero unresolved critical seams, slivers, wrong holes or broken connections | Every output plus complete source regression |
| Delivery | All requested slots accounted for; at least 95% complete valid pairs inside the frozen end-to-end deadline | Catalog and both novel batches independently; failed, refused, uncertain and timed-out requests remain counted |
| Evaluation | Critic approval precision at least 95% against a separately sealed AI panel, critical-defect recall at least 90%, decision coverage at least 80%; controls consistent | At least100 independent sealed stimuli, including at least80 naturally generated candidates, at least20 confirmed critical generated defects and at least20 predicted generated approvals; presentation repeats are extra |
| Repair | At least16 successful repairs out of20 separate confirmed repairable failures; no critical regressions | Frozen repair population across multiple construction classes, separate from critic controls and route selection |
| Generalization | Both sealed20-family batches pass all applicable gates independently | 80 outputs per batch, 160 total; no entire-family/derivative exposure in accessible generation inputs |

**Aggregation contract:** define an output's craft as the median of its independent ratings; then compute population medians across outputs. Keep each individual rating and disagreement. For parity, compute paired differences per reviewer on the same presentation protocol, aggregate within output, then across families. Missing, uninspected, rejected and unresolved outputs cannot be successes. Report craft medians with the requested-output ship/recognition rates so censoring cannot inflate quality.

**Uncertainty contract:** publish counts and family-clustered intervals with a recorded method, seed and resampling count. The thresholds above are empirical acceptance thresholds, not requirements that a 95% confidence lower bound exceed95%. Do not make the latter claim from20 families. A20-output stratum needs at least19 successes; a100-output audit stratum needs at least95. Supplemental morphology audits remain separate from the probability sample; do not pool them unweighted into a population estimate.

**Disagreement contract:** an identified critical defect quarantines the output. A factual conflict triggers one bounded evidence clarification tied to visible regions; it does not automatically redraw geometry or average a rejection into acceptance. Unresolved disagreement is uncertain and reduces coverage/yield. An author's self-review never counts as an independent acceptance vote.

**Calibration contract:** the score anchors must allow real house controls to exercise the same absolute craft threshold. Diagnose the A1579/8 exact-image case on development controls before freezing the critic. Do not add a score offset, suppress a strict reviewer, or lower the threshold to turn already observed candidates green. If the instrument cannot consistently apply the rubric to matched house controls, it cannot support a10/10 claim.
