## Outcome and scope

Generate professional, reference-consistent icons through the local CLI, with editable constrained programs, reliable filled cutouts, native-size proofs, and an honest acceptance decision. Target all seven rubric dimensions independently; a high engineering score cannot compensate for weak drawing.

Whole-library scope: every Blode concept belongs in the coverage denominator, outlined and filled, at native16px and24px. Full source inventory and reconstruction regressions cover every file. New-generation evaluation must span measured morphology strata and semantic families; cloud is one regression case, never the proxy for library quality. The20-family development cohort is the initial sample, not the scope boundary. Other families require their own qualification. No public web UI, backward-compatibility layer, package publishing, or training from scratch is part of this work. Preserve historical experiment artifacts as evidence without maintaining their runtime compatibility.

The current 5.0/10 assessment is a developmental judgment, not a blind benchmark. “10 across the board” means meeting the explicit release gates below on a frozen test population. It is not a promise of perfect outputs for every possible concept.

## Evidence that determines the approach

- Compiler16 corrected actual subtraction defects: overlapping cutters no longer restore ink, and boundary-crossing holes cannot add ink. D094 records 1,594 passing tests in 122 files, zero skipped. This establishes construction correctness for tested cases, not visual quality.
- The rounded Fable cloud remains a useful failing case: rounding its cutter does not round the new intersections with the cloud boundary. The repair in D096 was manually authored, not an autonomous Fable improvement.
- Blode supports both a solid cloud with an open negative arrow and an open cloud with a positive external arrow. The generator must select a reference-consistent construction, not enforce one polarity universally.
- The model screen compared first-pass outputs without the complete reference/parts/repair pipeline. It did not establish a best model. The image-assisted jellyfish run confounded sketch input with additional instructions and repair.
- Existing critics recognize some concepts but make incorrect claims about weight, caps, spacing, and pixels. Unqualified review must remain uncertain; it cannot approve craft or supply a training reward.

## Rubric and release gates

These are proposed acceptance thresholds to freeze before running the benchmark. Report counts, denominators, abstentions, and uncertainty alongside scores. No weighted average can qualify a failing row.

| Dimension | Current judgment | Required release evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition | 6/10 | At least 95% correct blind recognition in each paint and size; use predefined acceptable synonyms and unknown/distractor controls. No accepted icon conveys a conflicting action. |
| Visual craft | 4/10 | At least 95% of generated outputs are approved without manual geometry edits; every delivered output is explicitly approved. Human median craft rating at least 9/10, with no accepted sharp slivers, broken tangencies, accidental seams, or unbalanced counters. |
| Reference/style matching | 4/10 | At least 95% pass blind family-fit review against admitted Blode specimens; zero unexplained cap, radius, weight, or modifier-polarity violations among accepted outputs. Review both paints as a family. |
| Small-size legibility | 5/10 | At least 95% pass at each native size on light and dark backgrounds. All accepted modifiers/counters remain distinct at 16px; no acceptance based only on enlarged previews. |
| Geometry/SVG reliability | 8/10 | Every delivered artifact parses, conforms, and exactly replays. All regression cases pass; invalid/infeasible operations fail atomically. No background-colored fake holes, topology defects, clipping surprises, or silent recipe loss. |
| Autonomous evaluation/repair | 3/10 | On sealed human-labeled controls, at least 95% precision for automatic approval, at least 90% recall of critical defects, and at least 80% automatic-decision coverage. Identical and reversed-order controls must remain consistent. On a separate repair set, resolve at least 80% of repairable failures within the fixed budget without critical regressions. |
| Efficiency/predictability | 4/10 | At least 95% of requests finish within the declared time/attempt budget, with accurate terminal status and complete usage receipts. Provisional local target: p95 at most 10 minutes per concept pair. Choose the lowest measured cost route meeting every quality gate; fix its cost ceiling before final qualification. |

Craft and family fit require human audit until an instrument qualifies. No automatic critic can award itself 10/10. If the time or cost target proves impractical, report failure and an explicit proposed tradeoff; do not silently relax it.

## Execution sequence

### 1. Freeze the baseline and audit pack

Create a versioned benchmark manifest and a local artifact exporter using `scripts/local-style-run.ts`, `scripts/style-check.ts`, `scripts/review-proof.ts`, and the evidence boundaries in `scripts/local-author-evidence.ts`. Extend the existing mechanisms; do not build a second generation harness.

- Development: 20 concept families spanning cloud/modifier failures, rounded cutouts, intersecting strokes, organic silhouettes, dense details, and asymmetric forms. Include the frozen Astra/Fable/Gemini cloud programs unchanged.
- Judge calibration: separate human-labeled examples with subtle defects, identical pairs, reversed order, missing concepts, and valid unusual compositions.
- Qualification: two sealed batches of 20 unseen concept families, each in both paints and both native sizes. Seal before author/prompt tuning; exclude whole concept families, aliases, reference targets, and near duplicates from development and retrieval. Audit exclusions by source identity and geometric similarity. Report reconstruction separately.
- Every artifact carries concept, family/spec/compiler version, model and settings, reference hashes, program, SVG, native PNGs, findings, attempt history, elapsed time, and usage. Label manual repairs explicitly.
- Produce contact sheets and individual PNG/SVG files for user audit, with randomized IDs and a separate provenance key. The review packet must open locally without a public site.

Acceptance: reproduce known failures through the real renderer; record the initial rubric without inventing missing scores. Benchmark infrastructure refuses incomplete evidence and excludes no failures after seeing results. The user supplies craft labels through the audit packet; automated geometry work can continue while labels are pending.

### 2. Deliver genuinely rounded cutouts

First vertical slice: generate the Fable-style open cloud with a positive arrow, smoothly rounded roof and lower cut ends, and preserved cloud mass at 16px. Also retain the solid-cloud/negative-arrow variant as a valid reference-led option.

Use `src/tools/boolean.ts`, `src/tools/stroke.ts`, `src/geometry/rounded-line.ts`, `src/tools/canvas.ts`, and `src/tools/dsl.ts`. First determine whether explicit rounded terminal operands and existing subtraction express the intended contour. If they do, improve reusable construction recipes. If they do not, add the smallest explicit host-owned boundary-fillet capability, with family-tier radii and a serialized selection of affected corners. Do not globally smooth the result or let the model emit curve handles.

The contract must define concave versus convex corners, tangent joins, short-edge feasibility, protected contours, and topology preservation. Reject infeasible radii and self-intersections atomically with actionable errors. Computed tangencies remain host geometry; never snap derived control points independently. If modifier clearance needs a true offset, prove that gap separately before adding one; scaling a cutter is not an offset.

Acceptance: cloud, bell, shield, folder, and overlapping-cutout fixtures pass independent mask/raster checks, topology and tangent checks, DSL/document replay, and native visual audit. Include acute intersections, duplicate cutters, reversed winding, curved boundaries, full erasure, and infeasible radii. Keep before/after images from identical inputs. A manual fixture proves the capability; a fresh autonomous run must demonstrate the generator can use it.

### 3. Make reference-led composition repeatable

Automatic local retrieval is now implemented with explicit library configuration: semantic family discovery, image-grounded source selection, native component admission, and immutable author/reviewer context. Receipts retain every selection and refusal. Three development retrieval cases cover shield-check, folder-lock and bicycle; semantic family isolation remains unverified (mountain-bike is a bicycle donor). This closes manual reference selection for configured runs, not whole-library craft qualification.

Extend the current author context and shared skill (`scripts/local-author-context.ts`, `packages/iconsmith/SKILL.md`, packed `src/pipeline/skill.json`) and existing part retrieval. Pin a small admitted Blode reference set with provenance and family parameters. Keep whole-icon reconstruction, reusable parts, and novel generation distinct.

Have the author choose an explicit construction: body, counter, modifier, attachment, and clearance. Describe the modifier's polarity and relationship to the body before drawing. Retrieve relevant parts and use the existing constrained primitives for novel combinations. Avoid encoding a cloud-specific exception as a universal rule.

Acceptance: held-out development concepts beyond clouds improve under a matched before/after comparison with the same model and budget. Both paints preserve meaning and family character; neither is blindly derived by filling or stroking the other. Fresh programs replay and the user can audit actual images.

### 4. Make optical quality survive native sizes

Extend the existing size/spec and proof paths (`src/pipeline/style.ts`, `src/pipeline/pair.ts`, `src/tools/proof.ts`, and `scripts/style-check.ts`). Pin family-approved native-size parameters for weights, gaps, detail roles, and extents. Decide from measured proofs whether an icon needs a separate 16px construction; do not call a downsampled 24px rendering a calibrated optical master.

Acceptance: native 16/24 light/dark proofs for every development pair; small counters stay open and arrow direction stays clear. Diagnostics report painted geometry, not centerline bounds. Independent raster measurements support, but do not replace, visual review.

### 5. Qualify the judge and close the repair loop

Extend `src/eval/foundry-gate.ts`, `scripts/local-review.ts`, `scripts/review-construction.ts`, and `scripts/local-style-run.ts`. Separate deterministic conformance, recognition, craft, and family fit. Reviewers see actual hashed images and admitted references, not the author's explanation or model identity.

Require localized defects with observed evidence and uncertainty. A failed qualification keeps `craftApproved` false and routes output to human audit. Freeze judge thresholds on calibration data, then test on sealed controls. Do not use embedding similarity as craft approval.

Use the existing bounded author loop: at most three author attempts, a shared wall-clock deadline, preserved best candidate, and explicit stop on repeated defects, regression, or representation failure. Classify repairable drawing mistakes separately from absent capabilities. Repairs must target a named defect and rerender before acceptance.

Acceptance: meet the judge and repair rubric gates on separate sets; retain all failed attempts and abstentions. A critic that calls identical images different, fabricates spatial facts, or merely approves everything cannot qualify.

### 6. Choose the model route with a controlled comparison

After steps 2–5, compare currently accessible Astra, Fable, and Gemini candidates on the same development tasks, reference retrieval, tool contract, repair budget, and output requirements. Record exact callable model identities and access paths before any run. Prior screen results are screening evidence only.

Start with a small matched screen; expand only contenders. Compare vector-only against image-assisted composition with everything else held constant, including feedback. Preserve the original image for composition and keep it out of independent review. Add another provider only if a specific unresolved failure justifies the experiment. Do not repeat unsuccessful transports indefinitely.

Choose on quality first, then cost per accepted concept pair and p50/p95 latency, including failures and retries. Allow a conditional second route only if it demonstrably repairs a class the primary route misses. Image generation remains an optional composition source; final geometry remains constrained and editable.

Acceptance: a documented comparison that supports the selected route and discloses inconclusive differences. No claim that a vendor is universally best. Freeze the selected route and budgets before opening qualification batches.

### 7. Qualify delivery; train only for a demonstrated residual gap

Run the two sealed batches after freezing compiler, references, prompts, judge, route, and budget. Deliver randomized audit sheets plus individual images and provenance. Report each rubric row, every failure, automatic coverage, manual intervention, and actual cost. Both batches must meet every gate. A failure returns to the relevant slice; once opened, that batch becomes regression data and a new sealed batch is required for a new generalization claim.

Fine-tuning is conditional: only pursue it if repeated residual failures are model construction choices despite adequate geometry, references, and feedback. Curate human-approved input/decision/program demonstrations with family-level splits. Verify current provider support, data rights, access, and budget before reserving a training run. Keep the base-model comparison matched and require held-out uplift. A larger training corpus or model trained from scratch is not the default answer to a geometry defect.

Acceptance: publish a qualification report only when evidence supports it. Until then report the actual score and failing dimensions; never round an unfinished pipeline up to 10.
