## How to maintain this log

- Append each material decision and every attempt, including rejected, interrupted, misconfigured and zero-cost attempts, before ending the work session. Record intent before a paid call; append the actual outcome afterward.
- Give entries stable IDs, date, hypothesis or decision, rationale, alternatives, actual configuration, evidence paths, outcome, cost or unknown charge, and next action. Identify generated, retrieved, reconstructed and host-repaired artifacts separately.
- Record reservations before spending. Settle against provider receipts, including failed calls and reviews. Unknown charges retain their reservation and stop further paid work until reconciled. Never bill an old generation again when only reviewing it.
- Keep original artifacts immutable. Corrections are new entries with parent IDs and reasons; superseded decisions stay readable. Do not rewrite a failed attempt as a success after repairing its program.
- Retain small summaries and receipts under `docs/log/`. Large or third-party originals remain in their existing ignored directories; link them and record hashes. Hashes detect changed/missing evidence, but are not an asset backup. Do not move the corpus or stage vendor bundles.
- At handoff, reconcile the log against the budget ledger and new artifact directories. If evidence is unavailable, say so. Do not reconstruct missing scores, costs or chronology from guesses.

The [round-one evidence snapshot](log/foundry-round-1.json) retains configurations, costs, actual audit results and hashes for each run's files, even if local `.staging` files later disappear. Full plans and source discussions remain in the linked documents below.
